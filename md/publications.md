**Improving Fine-Grained Manipulation through Demonstration Expansion and Execution Design**\
**Jingxu Gu**, Dongze Lian\
*arXiv preprint, October 29, 2026 · RoboFineMani Workshop Challenge at CoRL 2026*

We present Demonstration Expansion and Execution Design (DEED), our approach to the MetaFine Open Competition at the RoboFineMani Workshop, CoRL 2026. DEED adapts a pretrained vision-language-action policy to the five MetaFine tasks through structured demonstration expansion and execution design, achieving an official Overall score of **85.55%**.
